Frequently asked questions

Short answers. The full rulebook lives in the FAQ.

How to start? +

Pick a challenge or an instant account, create a profile, and trade inside the simulated environment under the published rules.

I passed. What now? +

You get steps to open the funded simulated account and can request rewards once the cycle rules are met.

Can I trade news? +

Depends on the account. High-impact windows can be restricted. Check the objectives for your path.

Who is a funded trader here? +

Someone who cleared the evaluation, or who is on the instant path, and trades for a performance reward.

Can I change platform or size? +

Platform is chosen at checkout. Size changes follow the comparison table, not a silent upgrade.

Overnight or weekend holds? +

Allowed on some products, blocked on others. The objectives page is the source of truth.
